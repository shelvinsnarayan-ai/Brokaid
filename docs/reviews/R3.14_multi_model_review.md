## Feature summary given to the reviewers

> brokAId is an AI-assisted insurance broker platform (Australia). Two surfaces: the client app (/c, mobile-first, bottom bar Home/Activity/+/Messages/Profile)
> and the broker app (/o: AE, Principal, Operator roles). Built features relevant here:
> - Client: Home with cover cards and attention items; policy detail (what is covered, sum insured, excess, period); Activity with payments due and Pay;
>   + sheet (certificate, change cover, claim, documents); certificate of currency requests naming a holder; change-of-cover requests; claim lodgement;
>   claim tracker with stages, "What we need from you", and a Repair quote card (repairer, total incl GST, View quote, fixed insurer disclaimer);
>   Messages: broker thread and per-claim threads with photo/video attachments (metadata stripped) and broker PDF attachments (e.g. repair quotes);
>   Profile, Documents, Notification settings, Security (password + authenticator); renewal review/accept; client roles incl. bookkeeper/contact.
> - Broker: AE inbox and claim Messages panel (reply with photos/PDFs, labelled files); Principal approval of advice-like replies including the exact files;
>   queue/Today with endorsement approvals; client 360 view; renewal pipeline and review; claims list/detail with Documents (quote summary edit);
>   Operator Governance: Release Check, kill switches, exposure report; Alerts settings with a push test card (device checklist, test send, per-device result).
> Added since the R3.11 review (R3.12–R3.13c):
> - Client: policy page always has a Renewal section (state + "Review my renewal" / "Tell us about changes before renewal"); Home shows a
>   "renews in N days" card; renewal checklist asks to confirm sums insured and key values (changes become broker-reviewed requests).
> - Client: Profile → "People on this account": owner invites with presets (Bookkeeper, Co-owner, Read only, Custom), changes rights,
>   removes people; 72h single-use invite + authenticator enrolment; "Copy invite link" when email is off.
> - Client: Documents library grouped per policy (current schedule, previous schedules, PDS & wording, certificates, receipts & signed
>   acceptances, letters); "Ask your broker for a copy" when missing; "?" explainers (bottom sheet on phone, dialog on desktop) for
>   insurance terms; "What happens next" panels on change, claim, pay and certificate.
> - Broker: plain-sentence timelines (no raw ids/JSON for AE/Principal; Operator-only Details); alerts grouped by client and severity;
>   queue rows show client, what's needed, due by, Review, and a More toggle; Governance status cards with collapsible sections;
>   staff help tooltips and "How this works" playbooks; phone menu and stacked tables at 390 px on every staff route;
>   staff upload of policy schedules / PDS (PDF only, safety-checked, replace/withdraw, client notified); test data hidden from staff views.
> Not in scope of this evidence: anything not shown in the screenshots.
